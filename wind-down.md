First, prepare your mind. Count in your mind 10 things that went well. This gives you a sense of satisfaction before you go to bed. 
Second, prepare your body. Relax and tighten your muscles from head to toe. Each time, tell yourself that you feel great.
Third, give your mind a task. Stanford doctors recommend counting backwards from 600. You can recite an article, a mantra or massage your body from head to toe. The point is not to think about your life and give your mind a much deserved break. Avoid all excitement in this step, namely not to think about a movie, video game, novel, or a daytime event.
