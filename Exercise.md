We are surrounded by peers who exercise for hours. But when you have been feeling tired for some time, it is worth the while to look up information about overtraiing and pacing from creditable sources. Many people in the CF community consider over-training as the number one cause of relapse. Please don't take it lightly. 

Please see pacing here: https://www.cfsselfhelp.org/pacing-tutorial

Please read about the harm of overtraining here: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/overtraining-syndrome

Less is more.
